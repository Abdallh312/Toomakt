## 1. Project Concept & Brand Vision

### 1.1 What is toomakt?
**toomakt (توماكت)** is an Egyptian artisanal luxury confectionery atelier founded in Cairo. It was conceived as a deliberate rebellion against industrial, mass-produced candies that rely on artificial colorings, synthetic flavor syrups, palm oil, and high-fructose corn syrup.

toomakt brings back the timeless craft of **18th-century French confiserie**, marrying old-world copper-kettle cooking techniques with vibrant orchard harvests, pure fruit purées, and 84% butterfat European cultured butter.

### 1.2 Core Mission & Philosophy
- **Real Food Over Synthetic Flavorings**: Every fruit toffee is infused with cold-macerated real orchard fruit (wild strawberries, Alphonso mangoes, citrus zest) and natural pectin rather than artificial extracts.
- **European Dairy Purity**: Zero hydrogenated palm oils. Every batch uses 100% sweet cream cultured butter, delivering a rich, natural melt on the palate.
- **The Non-Sticky Confectionery Guarantee**: Engineered through precise temperature curves to ensure the chew never sticks uncomfortably to teeth.
- **Accessible Everyday Luxury**: Delivering high-end European boutique quality directly to Egyptian consumers and gifting connoisseurs at an honest price point.

### 1.3 Key Brand Innovations & USPs
1. **The Proprietary 45-Second Signature Chew Curve**: A multi-sensory chew that transitions from warm melting butter to an explosive burst of tart fruit acid, concluding in a clean, caramelized salted finish.
2. **"عيلة توماكت" (The Toomakt Family)**: Four flagship confection categories engineered to cater to diverse palate preferences (Fruity Candies, Butter & Milk Toffees, Coffee & Cappuccino Bonbons, and Molten Chocolate Eclairs).
3. **Nationwide Insulated Cold-Chain Delivery**: Egypt’s first confectionery brand to deploy insulated thermal cooler box shipping, guaranteeing fresh atelier-to-door delivery across all 27 Egyptian governorates without heat damage.
4. **Bilingual Seamless Experience**: Native full-spectrum Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR) interface reflecting authentic Egyptian heritage and global luxury standards.

## 3. Full Website Ecosystem & Application Views

The toomakt web application is a complete, feature-rich single-page application (SPA) with dedicated multi-view routing:

### 3.1 Shop All Catalog View (`#shop`)
- Full catalog display of all individual packs, curated duos, and collector bundle boxes.
- Category filtering (*All, Fruity, Butter & Cream, Coffee, Chocolate Eclairs*).
- Interactive cards featuring flavor notes, chewy ratings, pieces-per-pack info, and quick add-to-cart.

### 3.2 Slide-Over Tasting Bag (Cart Drawer)
- Slides in smoothly from the right (or left in RTL mode).
- **Free Shipping Progress Meter**: Real-time progress bar towards the 150 EGP free shipping threshold.
- Interactive quantity adjusters, item removal, and subtotal calculation.
- Coupon code redemption field (e.g., `SWEET10`, `TOOMAKT2024`).
- Direct checkout button leading to the checkout experience.

### 3.3 Product Detail Quick-View Modal
- Detailed high-resolution imagery and packaging previews.
- Full ingredient transparency and allergen declarations.
- Detailed sensory dials (Chewiness Score, Fruit Density, Roast Intensity).
- Pack size selection and direct "Add to Bag" action.

### 3.4 Multi-Step Egyptian Checkout View (`#checkout`)
- **Governorate-Smart Logistics**: Dropdown covering all **27 Egyptian governorates** (Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, Mansoura, Red Sea, etc.) with automated shipping fee calculation.
- **Egyptian Phone Validation**: Pre-configured for Egyptian mobile formats (`010`, `011`, `012`, `015`).
- **Flexible Payment Methods**:
  - Cash on Delivery (COD) with verification.
  - Online Card / Electronic Wallet readiness.
- Delivery notes for couriers and gifting recipient notes.

### 3.5 Order Confirmation & Receipt View (`#order-success`)
- Detailed order receipt featuring unique order reference ID (e.g., `TMK-89241`).
- Itemized breakdown of sweets purchased, discounts applied, and delivery governorate.
- One-click **PDF Invoice Download** capability.
- Instant links to live order tracking.

### 3.6 Live Order Tracking Portal (`#track`)
- Self-service portal for customers to look up delivery progress using their **Order ID** or **Phone Number**.
- Live visual progress bar: *Order Received → Simmering & Packing in Atelier → Handed to Cold Courier → Out for Delivery → Delivered*.

### 3.7 Wholesale & Corporate Gifting Portal (`#wholesale`)
- Dedicated B2B inquiry page for Egyptian corporate gifts, weddings, hospitality, and retail distribution.
- Tiered discount pricing matrix (50+ units, 200+ units, 500+ units).
- Inquiry form with company name, quantity needed, custom branding requests, and automated backend routing.

### 3.8 Editorial CMS Pages
- **Our Story (`#our-story`)**: Extended narrative on the copper kettle history and founder vision.
- **Ingredients & Purity (`#ingredients`)**: Detailed sourcing breakdown of European butter, fruits, and chocolate.
- **FAQ (`#faq`)**: Answers regarding shelf life (12 months), storage in hot weather, vegetarian certifications, and delivery timelines.
- **Shipping & Cooler Policy (`#shipping`)**: Deep dive into the thermal packaging insulation technology.

### 3.9 Admin Management Dashboard (`#admin` & `#admin/login`)
- **Authentication**: Secure token-based admin login.
- **Live Business KPIs**: Total Revenue (EGP), total orders count, average order value, pending fulfillment tally.
- **Order Management**: Filter orders by status (*Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled*).
- **Order Details Drawer**: View customer phone, address, items, and trigger status updates.
- **Invoice & Notification Dispatch**: Regenerate PDF invoices or dispatch automated updates to customers.

### 3.10 Floating WhatsApp Concierge
- Persistent animated WhatsApp button in brand-green with quick action prompt:  
  *"Hello toomakt Atelier! I have an inquiry."*
- Enables instant human assistance for VIP orders and custom packaging.

## 4. Technical Architecture & Tech Stack

### 4.1 Frontend Stack
- **Framework**: React 18 with TypeScript.
- **Build Tool**: Vite (blazing fast HMR, optimized production builds).
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom palette variables and RTL plugin support.
- **Motion & Interactions**: Framer Motion for spring physics, micro-interactions, and 3D card flips.
- **Smooth Scrolling**: Lenis smooth scroll engine for luxury editorial feel.
- **Celebration Effects**: Canvas Confetti for celebratory checkout moments.
- **Icons**: Lucide React.

### 4.2 Backend Stack (Python / Django)
- **Framework**: Django & Django REST Framework (`backend/store`).
- **Database**:
  - Local: SQLite (`db.sqlite3`).
  - Production / Cloud: Supabase PostgreSQL (`supabase_schema.sql`).
- **Key Modules**:
  - `views.py`: Robust endpoints for orders, products, inventory, and tracking.
  - `invoices.py`: Automated, printable PDF invoice generation via ReportLab.
  - `telegram_service.py` & `telegram_bot_daemon.py`: Real-time Telegram alerts to business owners upon every new order.
  - `whatsapp.py`: WhatsApp Business API integration for automatic order confirmation dispatches.
  - `supabase_sync.py`: Two-way synchronization between local Django DB and Supabase cloud.

### 4.3 Internationalization (i18n) Engine
- Built directly into `LanguageContext.tsx`.
- 550+ translation strings covering every label, product description, error message, and invoice field in both **English** and **Arabic**.
- Automatic document direction switching (`dir="ltr"` vs `dir="rtl"`).
- Dynamic typography switching: **Outfit / Inter** for modern English luxury; **Cairo / Alexandria** for elegant Arabic presentation.
